## Per-reference analyses (SEEN items)

### 1. Ro: "Zero tasks. You're all set."
- Problem: a home with nothing to do must not read as broken or empty.
- Observed: full-bleed misty lake photo; two lines of large light-weight sans, centred, the second line dimmer than the first (tone hierarchy, not weight). Two quiet tiles below ("Manage your care", "Help Center"), bell top-right, black brand block at the bottom.
- Why it works: the sentence is the whole screen; the calm backdrop carries the mood, so no badge or illustration is needed. Two escape routes keep it useful.
- Adapt: "nothing needs you" as a first-class home state: one declarative line plus a dimmed second line ("All three plants are fine." / "Tank has about 12 days left."). Use the paper plus line-drawn pot as backdrop.
- Do NOT copy: dark photo and glass tiles (breaks paper identity); "tasks" language, wrong for plants.

### 2. Workday: "You're all caught up."
- Observed: large empty white field, glossy 3D clipboard clip-art with smiley, bold sans headline, one grey explanatory line.
- Copy is clear and calm (state plus why). The generic clip-art is the template look; it says nothing about the product.
- Adapt: two-part copy. Replace the illustration with the brand pot.
- Do NOT copy: stock illustration, heavy bold headline.

### 3. Pool: notification primer
- Observed: duck mascot, white sheet with progress dashes (6 of 8 filled), back and Skip buttons, serif-ish heading "Get notified by Duck", four realistic example notification rows with timestamps, one "Give permission" button.
- Why it works: previews the actual notifications in the app's voice before the OS dialog; Skip is visible, so it is not coercive.
- Adapt: preview two or three real Plant Care alerts ("Fern is thirsty", "Tank under 15%"). Ask only once a plant or device exists. Show Skip as a plain text option.
- Do NOT copy: pale button fill that looks disabled; joke copy. Plant Care alerts are rare, say so ("a few a week at most").

### 4. Locket: calendar timeline and streak
- Observed: month cards of grey dot grids; one photo thumbnail where a day has a post; dashed connector between months; footer pill "8 Lockets | 1d streak" with flame.
- Why it works: dot calendar reads at a glance; empty days are dots, not warnings; the photo replaces a dot.
- Adapt: plant photo timeline as month dot grids with the photo where one was taken (maps to plant_photos); watering as small dots.
- Do NOT copy: flame streak counter (a missed day punishes the user; for a self-watering device a streak rewards the wrong behaviour); gold upsell pill.

### 5. Headspace: Today
- Observed: small "Good Afternoon, Ali", large "Start your day"; vertical dotted rail with one filled orange node (now) and hollow nodes (later); cards with colour illustrations and minutes; "329 meditating" social proof; lock icons on cards.
- Why it works: the rail says "this now, those later" without numbers.
- Adapt: rail only when something is due; small greeting above a large state line.
- Do NOT copy: lock icons, live social counters, saturated illustration on navy (generic wellness look).

### 6. Couple Joy: memories timeline
- Observed: dated left gutter (Dec / 17 / 2025, stacked small); photo card with title, two-line preview, comment and photo counters; second entry is a compact tinted one-line row ("We said 'I love you'  5 days ago", the number in accent colour).
- Why it works: two entry densities stop a timeline being monotonous; date gutter scans fast.
- Adapt: plant timeline with a date gutter; milestones (repotted, health check result) as one-line rows between photo cards.
- Do NOT copy: pink gradient CTA, emoji in titles (project rule), social counters.

## Comparisons

Sign-up first vs value first: Plant Care's value (identify a plant from a photo) could run before sign-up, but identification uses a per-user Plant.id quota and owner-only RLS, so it needs a user id. Compromise: pot, one line of promise and the camera step first; create the account at "Save this plant". I have no verified evidence of competitors' sign-up position (only Breathwrk step labels).

Permission up front vs in context: Pool primes with a realistic preview and a visible Skip, still inside onboarding. Notifications are only valuable after a plant or device exists, so ask after the first plant is saved or after pairing, with a preview of the real alerts. Camera: ask when the user taps scan; intent is obvious, no primer.

Gamified streaks vs quiet status: Finch and Locket are the gamified end (pet, levels, flame); Ro and Headspace are quieter (Headspace still adds a rail and social proof). With automated watering, a streak counter measures the wrong thing; show state ("fine", "tank 12 days") instead of counts.

Empty states: illustration plus cheerful headline (Workday) feels template-made; a single declarative sentence over a calm backdrop (Ro) feels intentional. For paper identity, the resting line-drawn pot is the backdrop.

Timelines: dot calendar (Locket) is best for glance; dated gutter with mixed-density entries (Couple Joy) is best for browsing. Use the dot strip as summary and the gutter list as the viewer.

## Identity lessons
- Hierarchy by tone and size, not weight: Ro's dimmer second line; Couple Joy's grey "Save Your" over white "Favourite Moments". Cheap and distinctive.
- One idea per screen with the sentence as the hero (Ro).
- Product-specific illustration beats stock clip-art (Workday reads template; Headspace's own characters read as brand). The pot drawing should appear in empty and error states, not only the launch.
- Real content in explainers (Pool's actual notification rows) instead of abstract benefit bullets.
- Copy with specifics and a consistent voice ("5 days ago" in an accent colour); avoid "Oops!" and exclamation marks.
- Restraint: avoid gradient CTAs, emoji, glassy tiles, lock icons.
- Caveat: these are my observations from six screens; I have no evidence about how they perform.
